A HEARTFELT INVITATION

For the 33rd time, the Jesus Youth Thrissur Zone family gathers under the shelter of divine grace.

Dear Jesus Youth family members!

December 13, Sunday • 12:00 PM to 5:00 PM

English

Once again, a blessed Christmas season is here!

The Jesus Youth family members in Thrissur are coming together once again in the sacred memory of Christmas. Let us wait in prayer and share the joy of Christ.

For Jesus Youth, these are days of divine grace and joy. Let us express our gratitude as the Holy See has granted definitive recognition to our movement. His promise to guide us step-by-step is being fulfilled through the times. Let us join hands to grow with greater commitment in the ministry of the Kingdom of God.

You are lovingly invited to our family gathering, which will be held on Sunday, December 13, from 12:00 PM to 5:00 PM at St. Paul’s School, Kuriachira.

PROGRAM VENUE & ROUTE MAP

Family Gathering 2026 will be hosted at the serene campus of St. Paul's C.E.H.S.S., Kuriachira, Thrissur. Easily accessible by car, two-wheeler, and local public bus service.

St. Paul's C.E.H.S.S., Kuriachira

🚌 Nearest Bus Stop 150m Walk

"Kuriachira St. Paul's School Stop" or Church Stop.

Open Turn-by-Turn GPS
1 From Thrissur Town (Sakthan / Swaraj Round)
3.5 KM • 8-10 Mins
  1. From Sakthan Thampuran Roundabout / Swaraj Round, proceed south along the Kokkalai road towards Koorkenchery.
  2. Cross the Koorkenchery Junction and continue straight onto Thrissur-Ollur Main Road (SH 22).
  3. At Kuriachira Junction, St. Paul's C.E.H.S.S. campus is located on the right side near St. Joseph's Church.
  4. Ample parking space is designated inside the school grounds for participants.
2 From NH 544 (Kochi - Salem Highway Bypass)
4.5 KM • 10 Mins
  1. Coming from Palakkad / Mannuthy side: Take the Kuttanellur bypass exit, drive via Ollur road directly towards Kuriachira.
  2. Coming from Kochi / Chalakudy side: Exit NH 544 at the Ollur exit (near Paliakkara), join Ollur town and proceed 2.5 km north to Kuriachira.
  3. Turn into St. Paul's School gate; Jesus Youth volunteers will guide you at the entrance.
🚌 Direct Local Bus Service to Kuriachira

Frequent private and KSRTC city buses connect Thrissur city stands directly to the school gate bus stop (every 3–5 minutes).

From Sakthan Thampuran Private Bus Stand:

Board any bus heading to Ollur, Chalakudy, Amballur, Puthukkad, or Kodakara.

From North Bus Stand / Vadakke Stand:

Board town shuttles marked Sakthan - Koorkenchery - Kuriachira - Ollur.

🚏 Designated Drop-off Bus Stop:

Ask the conductor for "Kuriachira St. Paul's School Stop" or "Kuriachira Church Stop". The venue is just a 2-minute level walk (150 meters) from the bus stop!
